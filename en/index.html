How we work

How the process runs

Five stages. You know in advance what happens at each one.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We go through the purpose of your trip, your dates and your circumstances in detail. Then we tell you which visa fits.

  2. 2

    Assessing your chances

    We tell you plainly where your documents are strong and where they are weak. If the chances are poor, we say so.

  3. 3

    Preparing the documents

    We draw up the checklist, complete the application form and bring translations and certificates up to standard.

  4. 4

    Booking the appointment and submitting

    We book your appointment at the consulate or visa centre and prepare you for the submission day and the interview.

  5. 5

    The decision and what follows

    We collect the decision together. If it is a refusal, we analyse the reasons and prepare a new submission.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a visa?

It depends on the country, the visa type and the consulate's queue. Preparing the documents usually takes a few days; the consulate's own processing can run to several weeks. We will give you the specific timeframe for your case at the consultation.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the visa type and the amount of work. We quote it in full before we begin and write it into the contract. Third-party costs such as the consular fee and translations are paid separately; we set those out in advance too.

What happens if the visa is refused?

First we work out why. Most often the file was incomplete, or the purpose of the trip was not backed up well enough. We fix the gaps and prepare a fresh submission.

Which documents do I need?

The list varies by country and visa type. The usual items are a passport, a photograph, a completed application form, proof of funds and letters establishing the purpose of the trip. We give you the full list for your case in writing after the consultation.

Can I apply if I have been refused before?

Yes. A previous refusal is not a permanent bar, but it does have to be taken into account. We look at your earlier application and the refusal note, and work out what needs to change next time.

Will I have to travel to another country to apply?

Some countries have no consulate in Turkmenistan, in which case the application is made in a neighbouring country. We tell you this in advance and help plan the trip so that it costs no more than it has to.
