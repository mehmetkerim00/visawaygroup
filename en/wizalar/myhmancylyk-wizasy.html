Visa type

Visitor visa

A visitor visa is issued for travelling to relatives or friends who live abroad. The key document is the invitation issued by your host, together with papers confirming your relationship and the purpose of the visit.

Who it is for

  • Anyone visiting relatives abroad
  • Anyone visiting on a friend's invitation
  • Anyone invited to a family occasion — a wedding or an anniversary
  • Anyone travelling to care for or help a relative
  • Anyone visiting their children or parents

The usual documents

  • Passport
  • A photograph of the required size
  • A completed visa application form
  • The host's invitationissued according to that country's rules
  • A copy of the host's ID and proof of their right to reside
  • Documents evidencing your relationshipbirth and marriage certificates — with translations
  • Details of where you will stayyour host's home, or a separate booking
  • Medical insurance
  • Documents showing your financial positionyour own funds or your host's sponsorship
  • Ticket reservations

This is a general list — the documents most countries ask for. The exact requirements depend on the country, the consulate and your own circumstances, and they change from time to time. We therefore draw up your specific list at the consultation and give it to you in writing.

Frequently asked questions

How is the invitation arranged?

The procedure varies by country: in some places the invitation is registered with a government office, in others a simple letter is enough. We give your host written instructions on what to do.

Do we have to be related?

No. An invitation from a friend is accepted too. In that case anything that evidences the friendship helps — previous visits, correspondence, photographs together.

Can my host cover the costs?

Yes, most countries have a formal procedure for this. Even so, you may still be asked for documents about your own finances.

What do I need if I am travelling with a child?

A child needs their own set of papers: a birth certificate, the notarised consent of the other parent if they are not travelling, and their own application form.
