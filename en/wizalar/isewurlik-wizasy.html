Visa type

Business visa

A business visa is issued for negotiations, trade fairs, training seminars and short business trips. The key document is the host company's invitation, together with letters establishing the business purpose of the visit.

Who it is for

  • Anyone travelling for talks with partners abroad
  • Exhibitors and delegates at trade fairs, forums and conferences
  • Employees sent on a business trip
  • Anyone travelling to accept equipment or oversee production
  • Anyone invited to a professional training seminar

The usual documents

  • Passport
  • A photograph of the required size
  • A completed visa application form
  • The host company's invitation
  • A letter or order from your own company about the trip
  • A letter from your employer confirming your job and position
  • The company's registration documentsif required
  • Confirmation of your registration for the fair or eventwhere relevant
  • Accommodation and ticket bookings
  • Medical insurance
  • Documents showing your financial positionyour own funds or company sponsorship

This is a general list — the documents most countries ask for. The exact requirements depend on the country, the consulate and your own circumstances, and they change from time to time. We therefore draw up your specific list at the consultation and give it to you in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I work abroad on a business visa?

No. A business visa covers short trips for business purposes only. To take up paid employment you need a work visa .

Is an invitation compulsory?

Usually yes. In some cases official confirmation that you are registered for a fair or event will also be accepted.

I have no company, I am a sole trader — what then?

That is fine. In that case we use your registration documents and letters evidencing your business relationships.

Can I get a multiple-entry visa?

In some countries, yes, usually where there is a history of previous trips. The consulate decides — it cannot be guaranteed in advance.
