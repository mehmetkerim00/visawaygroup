Study abroad advice

Study abroad advice

We help students who want to study abroad, from choosing a field and an institution through to submitting the application, being admitted and obtaining the student visa.

The decision to admit you is taken by the university itself. Scholarships and quotas depend on the programme, the application deadlines and the competition — nobody can guarantee them in advance.

Our job is to assess your chances realistically, find programmes that suit you, and prepare your documents on time and to the required standard.

Who it is for

  • School leavers who want to study abroad
  • Graduates planning a master's degree
  • Anyone changing career or taking a second degree
  • Anyone who wants to do a language course or foundation programme first
  • Parents choosing a field and a country for their child

The usual documents

  • School certificate or degree, with its transcripttranslated and, if required, certified
  • A language certificate or test resultif the programme requires one
  • Personal statementwhy this programme and this university
  • Letter of recommendationfrom a teacher or employer, if required
  • CV
  • Portfoliofor creative and architecture courses
  • A copy of your passport
  • Documents showing your financial positionfor tuition and living costs

Every university and every programme has its own requirements, and they change from year to year. The list above covers what is most commonly asked for. We draw up your specific list once the university is chosen, checking it against official sources.

How it works

  1. 1

    Assessing your profile

    We look at your grades, your language level, your budget and your goals, and tell you frankly what is realistic.

  2. 2

    Choosing a field and an institution

    We put together a shortlist of suitable programmes, with their requirements, deadlines and costs.

  3. 3

    Documents and personal statement

    We gather the documents, prepare the translations and write the personal statement together.

  4. 4

    Submitting the application

    We complete the forms, submit the documents on time and handle the correspondence with the university.

  5. 5

    Admission

    We wait for the replies, weigh up the offers together and help you decide.

  6. 6

    Student visa

    Once the offer letter arrives, we prepare and submit the student visa application.

  7. 7

    Getting ready to leave

    We explain what to do about accommodation, insurance, tickets and the first days after arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a scholarship?

Scholarships and fee reductions exist, but they are awarded competitively and the terms differ from programme to programme. We give no guarantees. What we do is show you where you have a realistic chance and prepare your application accordingly.

My language level is weak — do I have a chance?

Yes. Many countries offer language courses or foundation programmes — you start there and move on to the main degree. We discuss this when we assess your profile.

Which university can I get into?

It would be wrong to answer that in advance. First we look at your grades, your language and your budget, then we draw up a shortlist. The admission decision belongs to the university.

Do you handle the student visa as well?

Yes. Once the offer letter arrives we prepare the student visa application and stay with you right up to submission.
