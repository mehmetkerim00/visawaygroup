Visa type

Student visa

A student visa is issued for study at a university, college or language school abroad. Its key document is the offer letter from the institution, backed up by proof of funds for tuition and living costs.

Who it is for

  • Students admitted to a university or college abroad
  • Anyone going to a language course or foundation programme
  • Anyone starting a master's or doctoral degree
  • Anyone travelling on an exchange programme
  • School leavers continuing their studies abroad

The usual documents

  • Passportvalid for the whole period of study
  • A photograph of the required size
  • A completed visa application form
  • The offer letter from the institution
  • Proof of tuition paymenteither paid, or the agreed payment terms
  • Documents showing your financial positionyour own account, or your sponsor's documents
  • Your previous education documentsschool certificate, degree and transcripts, with translations
  • Evidence of your language levelif required
  • Medical insurance
  • Details of your accommodationhall of residence or rented accommodation
  • For applicants under 18 — the notarised consent of a parent

This is a general list — the documents most countries ask for. The exact requirements depend on the country, the consulate and your own circumstances, and they change from time to time. We therefore draw up your specific list at the consultation and give it to you in writing.

Frequently asked questions

I have no offer letter — what should I do?

You need to be admitted to a university or college first. If you want help choosing a field and an institution, we have a separate study abroad advice service.

Is a language certificate compulsory?

It depends on the programme. Some institutions set their own test, some require an international certificate, and language schools usually ask for neither.

Can I work while I study?

Some countries allow students to work a limited number of hours, others do not. The rule varies by country.

Is the visa issued for the whole course?

Usually not. The visa is issued for a set period and is then extended inside the country or converted into a residence permit.
